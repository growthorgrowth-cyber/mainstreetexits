Straight answers from a licensed broker

Main Street Exits exists to answer the question every owner eventually asks: what is my business actually worth, and how do I get that for it?

Main Street Exits is led by Eric Camehl, a Licensed Broker Associate with Infinity Business Brokers. We work with owners of established main street businesses across the country, from service companies and trades to retail, food and professional practices.

Most owners get their number wrong. They anchor to what they need for retirement, what a competitor claims they sold for, or what a calculator spit out. Then the business sits on the market for a year, or sells for a lot less than it should. We do it differently: real earnings, real comparable sales, and the same math a serious buyer and their lender will use. No inflated number to win your listing.

What we believe

  • Confidentiality is not optional. Your people and your customers should hear about a sale from you, at the right time, not from a listing.
  • Tell owners the truth. Including when the right move is to wait and fix something first.
  • A deal is not done until it closes. The work is getting it across the line, not just getting an offer.
  • Owners keep running the business. We run the sale.

What we do

  • Free confidential business valuations
  • Sell-side brokerage from pricing to closing
  • Exit planning for owners one to three years or more from selling
  • Buyer representation and acquisition help
