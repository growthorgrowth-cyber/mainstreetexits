## Content that earns links

- Update the "Ready to list?" checklist yearly and offer it as a free resource to CPAs, lenders and chambers.
- Publish one strong post a month answering real owner questions, and cite IBBA and BizBuySell data when it comes out.
- When IBBA Market Pulse or the BizBuySell Insight Report comes out each quarter, write a short "what this means for owners" post. Journalists and partners link to plain-English commentary.

## What not to do

No buying guest posts, no "link insertion" offers, no Fiverr backlink packages, no directory blasts, and no sitewide footer links swapped with unrelated sites.
