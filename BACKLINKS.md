## Dropped because they cost money

- **IBBA directory:** needs paid IBBA membership.
- **BizBuySell and BizQuest broker profiles:** BrokerWorks is a paid broker membership. Revisit only if Infinity's existing account can list Eric at no cost.
- **BusinessBroker.net broker profile:** couldn't confirm a free broker tier.
- **Chamber of commerce directories:** need member dues. Free chamber events are still worth attending for networking.
- **BBB accreditation:** a paid program.
- **Paid "councils", sponsored posts and link insertions:** paid placement.

## Content that earns links for free

- Update the "Ready to list?" checklist yearly and offer it as a free resource to CPAs, lenders and partners.
- Publish one strong post a month answering real owner questions, citing IBBA and BizBuySell data.
- When IBBA Market Pulse or the BizBuySell Insight Report comes out each quarter, publish a short "what this means for owners" post and share it with journalists who covered it.
