Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Main Street Exits ("we," "us," "our"), operating as a licensed broker associate affiliated with Infinity Business Brokers, collects, uses and protects information when you visit mainstreetexits.net, valuation.mainstreetexits.net, or contact us by phone, email or text message.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, state, industry, revenue range, sale timeline and anything you share in messages, calls or documents.
  • Business information: financial statements, tax returns and other records you choose to send for a valuation or sale.
  • Automatic information: basic technical data such as browser type, device, pages viewed and referring site, collected by our website and form providers through cookies and similar tools.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare a valuation
  • To provide brokerage, exit planning or buyer services you ask for
  • To send you texts and emails you have agreed to receive
  • To screen and qualify buyers and protect the confidentiality of sellers
  • To operate, secure and improve our website
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With Infinity Business Brokers, the brokerage we are affiliated with, as needed to provide services
  • With service providers who help us run our business (such as our CRM, form, email and phone providers), under obligations to protect it
  • With other parties to a transaction only with your permission, and in the case of buyers, only after they sign a non-disclosure agreement
  • When required by law or to protect our rights

Text messaging (SMS) privacy

If you opt in to receive text messages from Main Street Exits, we use your mobile number only to send the messages you agreed to. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP. See our Terms of Use for full SMS terms.

Confidential business information

We treat business financials and the fact that you are considering a sale as confidential. We release identifying information about a business for sale only with the owner's approval and only to buyers who have signed a non-disclosure agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep information as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal and recordkeeping requirements, then delete or de-identify it.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please do not email sensitive documents until we have discussed a secure way to share them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link, by emailing {{email}}. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will honor them as required.

Children

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
